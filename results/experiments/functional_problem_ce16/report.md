## Interpretation limits

The modes include uncertain-belief and known-problem controls, and learning/no-learning variants; pairing matches seed-level observation-noise innovations, not the endogenous observations or trajectories. CES and functional compliance are distinct outcomes. Cross-team contrasts are descriptive because initial competence geometry differs. A-versus-A recurrence is a path comparison, not an MIS-only effect. The four teams, one six-stage sequence, ten seeds, action set, and requirement rule bound all conclusions.
