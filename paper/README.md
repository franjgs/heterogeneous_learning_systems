# HLS architecture, theory, and validation paper draft

This is a working LaTeX skeleton, not a submission-ready paper. It frames HLS as an evolving competence organization, records a provisional architecture and theory scaffold, and preserves the internal minimal-model results audited in `docs/theory_competence_evolution_minimal_model.md`.

## Compile

From `paper/`:

```text
pdflatex main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex main.tex
pdflatex main.tex
```

The diagnostic figures are generated by `experiments/microverification/verify_minimal_theory.py` and are read from `../results/microverification/`.

## Structure

`main.tex` follows architecture/problem, integration theory, and validation: introduction; problem formulation; cross-domain foundations; provisional architecture; theory scaffold; minimal results; experimental methodology; limited microverification; discussion; and conclusion. `references.bib` contains only verified entries from the repository bibliography that are cited by this draft.

## Epistemic status and TODOs

CR0--CR4 are explicitly scoped to the minimal model. Numerical checks are consistency checks, not empirical validation. The architecture and competence-orchestration theory are CANDIDATE / WORKING HYPOTHESIS material. The draft contains no novelty claim and does not establish P4. Future work must supply a physically grounded competence-transition model, strong matched adversaries, and evidence before any general HLS claim can be made.

TODOs are intentionally substantive rather than cosmetic: establish a defensible integration theory, define physically meaningful competence transformations, and evaluate architecture-level hypotheses against strong matched alternatives in static and dynamic regimes.
