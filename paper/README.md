# Future HLS LaTeX manuscript

This directory is reserved for the future manuscript.  No substantive paper
is drafted at the pre-experiment foundation stage.

The manuscript may be created only after foundation review, preregistration of
the G00--G08 protocol, execution of the frozen campaign, retention and analysis
of positive and negative results, and targeted literature review.

The intended later layout is `main.tex`, `sections/`, `figures/`, `tables/`,
and `references.bib`.  Figures and tables must be generated from preserved
machine-readable results and versioned analysis, not manually transcribed
numbers.
