\section{Related work as importable apparatus}

This section is organised by scientific function rather than by claims of ownership. The methodological rule is to import mechanisms and mathematical tools without importing or replacing the HLS research problem.

\paragraph{Routing and model selection.}
Dynamic routing among heterogeneous models can trade quality against operational cost; RouteNLP, MixLLM, and related systems provide direct precedents for adaptive model selection and, in RouteNLP, targeted post-routing distillation \citep{guo2026routenlp,wang2025mixllm}. These works motivate strong routing baselines. They do not by themselves define a population-level competence state or establish a long-horizon HLS objective.

\paragraph{Learning and learner-aware intervention.}
Iterative Machine Teaching studies sequential interventions conditioned on learner behaviour or state \citep{liu2017iterative,liu2018blackbox}. Continual-learning work studies selective transfer and the possibility that transfer can help or harm different tasks \citep{lee2021sharing}. These are useful sources of intervention and transition mechanisms; they do not establish that a competence change should be valued through future operation of a heterogeneous portfolio.

\paragraph{Decision-aware allocation.}
Prediction-oriented and decision-targeted active learning distinguish uncertainty reduction from later decision value \citep{bickfordsmith2023prediction,filstroff2024targeted}. Graph-triggered rising bandits and decision-focused optimization provide examples in which state transitions, interactions, and final allocation quality matter \citep{genalti2024graph,elmachtoub2021smart,wang2023scalable}. They provide apparatus for later HLS models, not an equivalence claim between their system classes and HLS.

\paragraph{Competence development and division of labour.}
Portfolio, investment, organizational-competence, and control traditions motivate marginal value, rebalancing, option value, specialization, and path dependence. In this draft they are mathematical sources only. Similar mathematical structure does not demonstrate that the corresponding learning-system phenomenon has been established.

The cross-domain comparison therefore creates two obligations: extract reusable apparatus, and separately record the domain, assumptions, and evidence of each precedent. A mere combination of known components would not be a scientific contribution.
