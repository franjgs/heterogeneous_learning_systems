\begin{abstract}
This working draft develops a minimal analytical language for deliberate competence evolution in heterogeneous learning systems. It distinguishes exploiting a current division of labour from shaping the future distribution of competence through routing, experience, and learning. Under explicit two-learner/two-task assumptions, the draft records a separability null case, a finite competence threshold, a local specialization-instability condition, and a two-actuator rebalancing calculation. Symbolic and numerical checks validate identities of the minimal model only. The draft makes no novelty claim, does not establish a general result for real heterogeneous learning systems, and does not establish a coupling advantage over a fully informed modular controller.
\end{abstract}
