## 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05 — finite foundations and conditional integration

Source records for a static allocation ground-truth consolidation and dynamic competence-development validation were recorded in Git on 1 October; these are record dates, not verified dates of the original mathematical work. The static crossed-specialization construction derived a finite gain formula for task-wise allocation. The dynamic construction supplied cases where competence-state change leaves the efficient frontier unchanged and where future development value reverses an immediate preference. A G3-H analysis recorded on 4 October corrected a local opportunity argument: local action ranking reversal alone does not establish a root-level value gap. The joint/separated policy-class boundary recorded on 5 October gives a conditional class-inclusion result, not a claim that integration is generally superior.

## 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-15 — portfolio learning and initial laboratory campaigns

On 13 October, a record consolidated C2/C3 and C4 statuses; this did not make unfinished work confirmatory. ## 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-19 — dimensional expansion, audit and stop decisions

Between 16 and 17 October, N×K geometry, production, learning-by-doing, teaching, competence estimation and environment-generation components were developed and checked incrementally. MIS and MIS-v2 were explicitly retained as distinct learning laws rather than reconciled by name. These were engineering and bounded validation milestones, not proof of general adaptive performance. The 17 October adaptive-capability validation closed within a bounded integration scope. A literature-reproduction screening on 19 October found no candidate sufficiently specified for faithful execution and stopped before a new experiment. Separately, HLS-G0-C6 was closed STOP on 19 October: its finite gap was explained within the tested setting by anticipatory development followed by flexible routing; it was not a general structural result.

## 2026-10-24 — deliberation feasibility and AI-aging stop

The PI proposed a priority order: dynamic organization of fast and deliberative capabilities; selective cooperation; then organizational learning/emergent specialization. The CE authorized a feasibility-planning task only, not an experiment, algorithm or manuscript. Existing MMLU records compared independent answers and did not include answer-conditioned review D(x,F(x)); this ruled out treating them as evidence of sequential revision. The exact synthetic AI-aging control held individual error marginals at 0.20 while majority risk differed (0.30 versus 0.20) across two joint error laws, but this was not real-model evidence. CE-02 closed NO-GO because originality and a suitable real configuration were not established; it did not declare aging irrelevant.

## 2026-10-24 to 2026-10-26 — transfer hypothesis and incremental research directive

CE-03 tested whether transfer could improve a receiver while reducing collective performance under a fixed organization. The first campaign was followed by a diagnosis of small transfer and reorganization effects. V2 compared supervised updating, distillation and a permuted-prediction negative control; the reported intervals did not establish a reliable distillation advantage or collective harm. This did not confirm H3. On 26 October the PI directed incremental study of HLS dimensions, adequately sized organizations, simple scalable scenarios, competitive baselines and measurement of individual, collective and cost outcomes. A subsequent CE-05 literature/mathematical review rejected an initial O1 framing and corrected the programme away from originality-first screening.

Evidence references: archive docs/research/HLS_CE_03_H3_CAMPAIGN_RESULTS.md, docs/research/HLS_CE_03_H3_V2_CAMPAIGN_RESULTS.md, docs/HLS_HISTORICAL_MEMORY_AND_NOVELTY_GUARD.md and docs/research/HLS_SCIENTIFIC_NOVELTY_AUDIT_2026-10-19.md. ## 2026-10-26 — deadline allocation, heterogeneity and joint structure

CE-04 studied fixed competence profiles, assignment policies and scale in a synthetic deadline queue. Across its 1,080 confirmatory simulations, H2 exceeded H0 under the information-complete policy by about 11–12 percentage points at N=12, 24 and 48. Estimated competence lagged the true-competence reference; contextual adaptation added a scale-dependent increment. The result is scoped to its scenario and policies, with no learning or cooperation; it does not prove P1 globally optimal or establish a real-workplace effect. CE-06 reanalyzed the same 90 N=24 P1 replicas and added descriptive selection/load measures; it is not an independent replication and does not identify congestion mediation.

CE-07 then compared two competence matrices with equal per-function marginal mean and maximum in one stationary synthetic queue under fixed P1. The valid 30-seed confirmatory comparison favored the specialist structure by 21.52 percentage points in success-on-time. The first attempted pilot/campaign had nonstationary family demand contrary to protocol and was excluded. The valid result supports only a difference between those two structures in that model; mediator and generalization claims remain unidentified.

## 2026-10-27 — compact scientific core and knowledge consolidation

The active branch was reconstructed as a compact scientific core rather than as a copy of historical campaign directories.
