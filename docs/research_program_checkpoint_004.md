# Research Programme Checkpoint 004

> **Historical checkpoint.** It preserves the programme state before the 2026-09-17 RQ0 and ontology consolidation. Its quoted RQ0 is not current; use [research_questions.md](research_questions.md) and [hls_ontology.md](hls_ontology.md).

Date: 2026-09-15
Status: programme-level competence-evolution model consolidated after cross-domain and minimal-theory audit; no general HLS claim, novelty claim, or validated realistic experiment.

## 1. Executive position

The programme is organized around the whole Heterogeneous Learning System (HLS), not a particular routing, teaching, transfer, or minimal-model mechanism. An HLS is treated as an evolving distribution of usable competences whose operational value depends on its environment and horizon.

## 2. RQ0

RQ0 remains the sole official research question:

> Can the deliberate evolution of competence allocation in a heterogeneous learning system improve its long-term performance compared with independently optimizing task allocation and knowledge transfer?

RQ0 remains a hypothesis. This checkpoint neither promotes P1--P4 nor changes the question.

## 3. Scientific object and current programme model

The scientific object is a heterogeneous portfolio of learners whose competence allocation may change over time:

```text
competence allocation
    -> division of labour / operation
    -> experience
    -> learning / knowledge transfer
    -> future competence allocation.
```

Competence allocation is a candidate system-level decision variable. The programme asks whether deliberately shaping who will be competent at what can create greater long-term system value than optimizing only current use and local improvement.

## 4. What has been learned so far

The landscape shows substantial precedent for components in routing, knowledge transfer, Machine Teaching, continual learning, resource allocation, control, and related areas. This neither closes the HLS question nor supplies a novelty claim. It directs the programme to import useful mechanisms and mathematical apparatus while keeping the scientific object at the HLS level.

The central distinctions are:

```text
individual failure        != portfolio competence deficit
individual improvement    != collective system improvement.
```

Knowledge or competence is the managed material; learning is the transformation mechanism; heterogeneity defines the portfolio; operation gives competence value; and time makes competence investment meaningful.

## 5. Role of P1--P4

P1--P4 are subordinate analytical scaffolding: collective competence, local--collective misalignment, evolution versus frozen routing, and a candidate coupling advantage. They help formulate comparisons and adversaries; they are not official RQs or established programme results. In particular, P4 is not established.

## 6. Role of cross-domain mathematics

Portfolio management, Operations Research, economics, optimal/stochastic control, organizational science, human competence management, and machine learning are sources of apparatus: state representations, transition laws, objectives, constraints, theorem patterns, and experimental designs. The rule is to import mechanisms and mathematical tools, not to import or replace the HLS research problem. Mathematical resemblance neither proves novelty nor proves that HLS is already solved.

## 7. Role of CR0--CR4

The minimal theory and microverification provide model-scoped consistency and falsification evidence. They establish specified separability, threshold, specialization, and rebalancing identities only under their explicit minimal assumptions. They do not establish general HLS behaviour, realistic empirical value, novelty, RQ0, P4, or superiority of a coupled controller.

## 8. Strong adversaries and null cases

Important comparisons include a sufficiently rich frozen heterogeneous portfolio with a strong adaptive router, reactive or local-improvement policies, and a fully informed modular controller. Null cases include free or trade-off-free competence acquisition, complete future information with an optimal ex-ante portfolio, competence transitions independent of operation, and transitions unable to affect future operational choices.

## 9. What is not established

There is no general HLS algorithm, validated realistic experiment, general competence representation, established novelty claim, or general proof that deliberate competence evolution is superior. The value of specialize, broaden, replicate, and rebalance remains conditional and unestablished outside defined models and experiments.

## 10. Experimental epistemology

Experimental outcome and validity must be recorded separately. A future study must specify its HLS proposition, what supports or weakens it, its strongest adversary, matched resources and information, confounds, and why the system exposes the proposed mechanism. Controlled synthetic work can isolate causes; realistic validation can test relevance. Neither alone establishes RQ0.

## 11. Current research direction

The next progress should strengthen, specify, or refute the general hypothesis through small physically meaningful competence transformations, operational environments, null cases, and fair strong comparisons. It should not merely extend a convenient lateral mechanism, model, or experiment.

## 12. Stop/go logic

Continue only while the general question can be made operational without vacuous assumptions and while fair comparisons leave meaningful conditions for deliberate competence evolution to matter. Redirect substantially if strong frozen, reactive, local, or fully informed modular alternatives remove the effect under conditions where the programme claims it should appear.

The programme-level message is:

```text
knowledge / competence is the managed material
learning is the transformation mechanism
heterogeneity defines the portfolio
operation gives competence value
time makes competence investment meaningful
the whole HLS determines the scientific objective.
```
