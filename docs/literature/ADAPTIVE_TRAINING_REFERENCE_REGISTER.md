## Reading status and next verification

- **Full text consulted for targeted mechanism claims:** JHT18 and NB15. JHT18
  explicitly models learning by doing plus learning from observing/interacting
  with co-located colleagues, and optimizes worker grouping and assignment.
  NB15 models experience and knowledge transfer in selection/grouping/
  assignment. These are close prior art for workforce transfer and allocation.
- **Full text present locally but not yet reviewed for all claims in this
  project:** BLY23 and BM24. Their summaries below rely only on verified
  abstracts/metadata unless a separate source note states otherwise.
- **Abstract/metadata only in this audit:** CHT17, CHT18, VHTB17, SZZ25, GD14.
  Mark unreported mechanisms as pending, not absent.
- Next review should read the full texts of all eight, prioritizing whether
  teaching effectiveness is relation-specific, initially unknown, updated
  online from observed outcomes, and jointly optimized with production and
  uncertain future demand.
