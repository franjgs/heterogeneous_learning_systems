# Literature Needs Before Manuscript Drafting

This is a review agenda, not a literature review.  Bibliographic metadata must
come from the audited `docs/literature/references.bib`; no new citation is
asserted here.

## A. Sources already identified in the repository

The retained bibliography and literature map already identify source families
for:

- organization of distributed knowledge and expertise allocation (including
  Garicano);
- learning by doing and task-specific human capital (including Arrow,
  Gibbons--Waldman, and Argote--Miron-Spektor);
- competence-oriented dynamic portfolio/project selection (Gutjahr and
  collaborators);
- assignment, training, learning by doing, and knowledge transfer in staffing
  settings (including Nembhard--Bentefouet and Borgonjon--Maenhout);
- transactive memory systems (Argote--Ren);
- Bayesian active/decision-focused learning sources retained for possible
  comparison.

These entries are leads. Their claims and fit must be rechecked against the
primary sources before use in manuscript prose.

## B. Targeted review questions still pending

1. **Team functional diversity and expertise configuration:** Which theories
   distinguish totals, interpersonal placement, complementary specialization,
   overlap, and redundancy?
2. **Adaptive teams:** How is adaptation distinguished from initial fit, team
   learning, and reconfiguration?
3. **Transactive memory/distributed expertise:** What does “who knows who knows
   what” require, and how should the current model's absence of TMS be framed?
4. **Problem representation:** Which literatures separate objective task
   structure, internal hypothesis repertoire, and model misspecification?
5. **Organizational learning/learning by doing:** Which qualitative premises
   support experience-to-capability development without implying MIS-v2's
   exact equation?
6. **Absorptive capacity:** Is it relevant to later transfer/reframing claims,
   or should it remain outside the current model?
7. **Environmental change and complexity:** How are rate, magnitude,
   recurrence, and novelty operationalized without conflating team difficulty?
8. **Finite/misspecified Bayesian representations:** What established results
   are needed to contextualize inference when `z*` is outside `Z_hat`?
9. **Exploration/exploitation:** Is the term appropriate for the current
   information-production tradeoff, and what distinctions are necessary?
10. **Prior ABMs of teams:** Which models jointly vary team diversity and
    task/environment structure, and what mechanisms do they include that HLS
    omits?
11. **History, practice order, and policy-mediated exposure:** Which literatures
    distinguish an intrinsically order-sensitive learning law from order
    effects generated because intermediate capability changes subsequent task
    assignment or practice opportunities? This review is required before the
    post-hoc Campaign 0 interpretation is given broader theoretical meaning.
12. **Prospective adaptive decision policies:** Campaign 2 compared frozen
    prospective restrictions inside one HLS operator. Which established
    decision-theory, active-learning, adaptive-control, and organizational
    learning constructs can legitimately contextualize Q10/Q01/Q11 without
    relabeling internal opportunity terms as causal “values” or strict dual
    control? The literature review must preserve the observed Q01 null and the
    failure of internal advantage to guarantee realized benefit.

## C. Claims requiring external support

- empirical prevalence and consequences of expertise configuration;
- qualitative learning-by-doing/diminishing-gain premises;
- relevance of finite or misspecified representations to human/organizational
  problem solving;
- interpretation of adaptive-team behavior and environmental dynamism;
- any claim of practical or ecological relevance;
- comparison to existing team/task ABMs.
- any empirical interpretation of cumulative exposure or policy-mediated
  capability path dependence beyond the frozen HLS model.
- any claim that the eight-scenario Campaign 1 range is representative of
  real task environments or sufficient for broad strategy ranking.
- any generalization of Campaign 2's conditional Q10/Q11 benefit, Q01 null,
  low-prevalence coupling, or G07 secondary dominance beyond the 15,360
  Q11-reference states and frozen test-range physics.

## D. HLS assumptions not to attribute falsely to literature

- `theta=(p,1-p)` and `Z=Delta^1` as the current problem ontology;
- `rho=.5`, `sigma=.10`, horizon three, and the discrete 64-action space;
- MIS-v2's exact exponential latent capability equation and common eta;
- the neutral output envelope and normalized sup distance `d_R`;
- descriptors `C_t`, `N_t`, and `M_t` as presently defined;
- reset of belief and persistence of `S` across problems;
- the six-stage Small Problem World and finite `Z_hat`;
- the two-step MPC approximation.
- the Q00/Q10/Q01/Q11 prospective ablation taxonomy, its opportunity
  decomposition, and the two-reward counterfactual endpoint.
- the cumulative-exposure identity as an algebraic consequence of MIS-v2; it
  should not be attributed to literature merely because exponential learning
  curves or learning-by-doing are discussed elsewhere.
