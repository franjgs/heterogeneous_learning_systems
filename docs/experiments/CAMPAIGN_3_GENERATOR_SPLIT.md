# Campaign 3 crossed generator split

## Status and provenance

This pre-history design freezes the generator-regime split after PGCG and
before generating any C3 development or confirmatory history. Kernel IDs are
derived from the frozen PGCG table; the derivation rule is authoritative over
manual transcriptions. No agent, reward, learning, policy, or performance
quantity enters the split.

## Crossed rule

The ten positive-MOVE compositions are sorted lexicographically by
`(alpha_STAY,alpha_MOVE,alpha_RETURN)`. Their held-out MOVE scales cycle
`L,M,H,L,M,H,L,M,H,L`, with `L=.01`, `M=.04`, and `H=.32`.

This gives:

```text
Phi_heldout = K00 K04 K08 K09 K14 K18 K19 K24 K28 K30

Phi_dev positive-MOVE =
K01 K02 K03 K05 K06 K07 K10 K11 K13 K15
K16 K17 K20 K21 K23 K25 K26 K27 K31 K32
```

Each held-out composition-by-scale combination is absent from development,
while its composition and its scale occur elsewhere in development. This is
**compositional/interpolative generalization to unseen generator kernels**, not
extrapolation or out-of-region generalization. Campaign 3 currently has no
contiguous structural-region holdout.

## Static quotient

`K12`, `K22`, `K29`, and `K33` remain separate parameterizations in PGCG
provenance but induce the same constant-history law under frozen initialization.
For coverage/generalization they form one dynamic regime represented by pure
STAY `K33`. The aliases `K12/K22/K29` are neither development nor held-out
treatments.

Therefore:

```text
Phi_dev = 20 positive-MOVE kernels + K33 = 21 dynamic regimes
Phi_heldout = 10 positive-MOVE kernels = 10 dynamic regimes.
```

## Future claims and data separation

Fresh independent histories from `Phi_dev` will address unseen-history
generalization for represented kernels. Fresh independent histories from
`Phi_heldout` will address compositional/interpolative generalization to unseen
kernels. These are distinct claims.

PGCG histories are permanently ineligible for training, predictor/selector
development, unseen-history evaluation, or held-out-kernel confirmation.
Future histories require fresh independent seeds. The split cannot change in
response to agent behavior, rewards, performance, selector results,
discriminative power, realized C/N/M, or perceived difficulty. No future C3
history is generated by this freeze.
