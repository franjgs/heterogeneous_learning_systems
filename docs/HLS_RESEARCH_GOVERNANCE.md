# HLS Research Governance

**Decision authority:** Chief Engineer (CE)
**Decision date:** 2026-10-24
**Record status:** Current organizational decision

This document assigns primary responsibilities for research work in HLS. It
formalizes work boundaries and handoffs; it does not change HLS's scientific
philosophy, research questions, theory, evidence, or laboratory architecture.
The [team handover protocol](team/HLS_TEAM_HANDOVER_PROTOCOL.md) continues to
govern role transfers and operational continuity.

## Primary responsibilities

| Role | Primary responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **E1 — THEORY** | Mathematical formulation, hypotheses, properties, proofs, and theoretical foundations. |
| **E2 — STATE OF THE ART** | Literature review, prior work, scientific originality, and research gaps. |
| **E3 — EXPERIMENTATION** | Experimental design, benchmarks, protocols, statistical analysis, and empirical validation. |
| **CODEX — SOFTWARE ENGINEERING** | Implementation, tests, infrastructure, integration, technical documentation, and Git. |
| **CE — CHIEF ENGINEER** | Scientific direction, coordination, prioritization, and GO/NO-GO decisions. |

## Changes and collaboration

The CE may change a primary responsibility through an explicit decision. The
decision, date, scope, and new assignment must be recorded in the repository;
until then, this register remains in force. A collaboration, review, or
contribution by another role does not by itself transfer or alter primary
responsibility. Work that crosses responsibilities should identify its
contributors while retaining the named primary owner unless the CE records a
change.
