# Validation report

Tests exercise continuous-calendar assignment parity on a deterministic fixture; no work before weekday opening; shift-end suspension and same-analyst resumption; preserved effective service; weekend continuity; and natural-time deadline classification.

The historical alpha=1 equivalence check is a useful regression reference for hidden-work realization, but does not establish availability-engine parity. Continuous-calendar equivalence must compare assignments, completion times, and metrics. Campaign-level gates must additionally compare paired demand, recommendations, needs, competencies, service times, label-blind dispatch inputs, and S_plus numerator/denominator. Treat results as exploratory until these gates and seed-precision planning are complete.
