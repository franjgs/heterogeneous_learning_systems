# Scientific implications

The question is how L2 specialist availability changes the operational consequences of SOCTRA triage. The calendar contrast can change deadline completion, sensitivity to false-positive workload cost alpha, relative D1/P1/WAPS performance, and classifier-by-organization interactions. Since 8x5 has lower weekly capacity than 24x7, effects combine capacity and temporal placement.

There is no completed campaign from which to recommend changing the article. Treat this as a follow-on robustness study. Consider incorporating results only after paired validation and evidence of stable, practically material changes in the primary endpoint or interpretation of classifier/policy comparisons. No policy winner, new policy, or fast/late escalation is introduced.
